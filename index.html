About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Chung-Ang University (CAU), where I am leading the Machine Intelligence & Data Science (MINDS) Lab. Prior to joining CAU, I worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Computer Science at University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) with Prof. Hanghang Tong. I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Hanyang University under the supervision of Prof. Sang-Wook Kim. My research interests mainly lie in data mining and machine learning on various types of data (including but not limited to graph, hypergraph, text, image, and time-series) with a special focus on exploring knowledge for real-world applications.
